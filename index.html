Still

Meeting notes that never leave your devices. For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  • Record meetings, lectures and calls. On Mac, Still captures the call and your microphone and ignores music and videos.
  • Transcribe on device, with timestamps and the name of who spoke.
  • Translate locally, and get topics, summaries, decisions and tasks from a local AI model.
  • Search by words and by meaning.
  • Sync through iCloud with end-to-end encryption, or directly between your nearby devices.
  • No account, no cloud AI, no analytics unless you turn diagnostics on.
