Common questions

Does Still send my recordings anywhere?

No. Recordings, transcripts and notes stay in an encrypted library on your device. With iCloud sync on, only encrypted data travels to your own iCloud account.

Why does Still download files during setup?

Transcription and the local AI model run on your device, so Still downloads them once after you approve. After that, Still works in airplane mode.

The Mac does not record the other side of a call.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and allow Still. Still records audio only, never the screen.

I lost my device.

Open Still on another device, go to Settings → Devices and revoke the lost one. Use your recovery code if no other device is unlocked.

How do I delete everything?

Delete recordings in Still, or delete the app. If you used iCloud sync, turn sync off first or remove Still data in iCloud settings.
